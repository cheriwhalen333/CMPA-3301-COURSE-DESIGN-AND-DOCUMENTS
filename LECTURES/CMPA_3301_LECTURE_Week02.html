Week 02: Execution & Infrastructure

Moving from the "Why" to the "How" of project execution.

🎯 Pillar I: Infrastructure as Process

In Project Management for the Unofficial Project Manager, the formula for success is VALUE + PEOPLE + PROCESS.

We often think of "process" as just meetings or schedules, but in digital work, infrastructure is your process. GitHub is not just a storage site; it is the physical architecture of your project's accountability. By using version control, you are practicing accountability—one of the Five Foundational Behaviors—because you are creating an unalterable history of every decision you make in your code.

🔧 Pillar II: GitHub.com vs. GitHub Desktop

A common point of confusion is thinking GitHub.com and GitHub Desktop are the same. They serve two different, essential roles in your "Hybrid" toolkit:

GitHub.com (The Cloud/Hub): This is your project's permanent home. It lives on the web, where teams collaborate, documentation is hosted, and your "Live" public URL exists.

GitHub Desktop (The Workbench): This is your local interface. It allows you to "sync" the project from the cloud to your local computer, edit files, and "commit" changes back to the hub without needing to know complex code.

⚠️ Finding the Tutorial

When you open GitHub Desktop for the first time, look at the startup dialog for "Create a Tutorial Repository...". Click that. It is the hidden key to this week's exercise. It will walk you through your first commit, branch, and pull request in a safe, sandbox environment.

💡 Pillar III: Informal Authority & Behaviors

You don't need a manager title to lead. In Chapter 2 of Project Management for the Unofficial Project Manager, the authors discuss the Five Foundational Behaviors for building informal authority:

  • Listen First: Seek to understand before you seek to be understood.
  • Clarify Expectations: Define "what success looks like" early.
  • Extend Trust: Empower others to act.
  • Practice Accountability: Own your output and support others in theirs.
  • Demonstrate Respect: Value the contributions of every team member.
